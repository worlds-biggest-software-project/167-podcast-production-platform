## Relevant Industry Standards or Protocols

- **RSS 2.0 with Podcast Namespace extensions** — the foundational distribution standard; Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and all major directories consume RSS feeds with episode metadata
- **LUFS loudness normalisation (EBU R128 / ATSC A/85)** — broadcast standard for podcast audio levels; all professional tools target –16 LUFS for streaming platforms
- **MP3 / AAC audio encoding** — dominant delivery codecs; MP3 for broad compatibility, AAC for better quality at lower bitrates on Spotify and Apple Podcasts
- **ID3 tags / Podcast 2.0 namespace** — metadata standards for chapters, transcripts, funding, and person tags enabling richer podcast clients
- **Podcast Index / Podcast 2.0** — open standards movement adding chapters, transcripts, value4value (streaming sats), and soundbite tags to the RSS ecosystem

## Market Research

**Market Size:** The global podcasting market is valued between $32–62 billion in 2026 (estimates vary widely by scope). The podcast hosting software sub-segment is more conservatively valued at approximately $420 million in 2024, projected to reach $1.2 billion by 2033 at a 12.5% CAGR. The broader podcasting economy — including ad revenues, paid subscriptions, and production services — is the more relevant total addressable market.

**Funding:** Riverside raised $35 million in a 2022 Series B. Descript raised $50 million across multiple rounds and was acquired by Spotify in 2023. Zencastr raised $20 million in a 2021 Series A before being acquired by Spotify as well, signalling platform consolidation.

**Pricing Landscape:** Recording and editing tools range from free tiers to ~$35–50/month at the high end. Hosting-only tools start at $12/month. Bundled all-in-one platforms (record + edit + host + distribute) are capturing market share from point solutions, with pricing in the $19–45/month range. AI features (show notes, chapters, clip generation) are shifting from add-ons to core features.

**Key Buyer Personas:** Independent creator-podcasters monetising audiences, branded podcast producers at marketing agencies, internal learning and communications teams producing episodic content, enterprise thought leadership teams, and media companies managing large show catalogues.

**Notable Trends:** Spotify's acquisitions of Descript and Zencastr signal platform consolidation. Video podcasting (recording and distributing video to YouTube alongside audio) is now expected rather than optional. AI-generated show notes, chapters, and social clips are table stakes in 2026. Podcast 2.0 namespace adoption is growing, enabling transcripts, chapters, and value4value payments natively in RSS.

## AI-Native Opportunity

- One-click post-production that ingests raw audio, applies noise removal, loudness normalisation, silence trimming, and filler-word removal without a human touching an editing timeline
- Automatic chapter and show-note generation from transcripts, formatted to match the creator's established voice and style
- AI clip selection that identifies the most shareable 30–90 second moments from a full episode and exports them in vertical, square, and landscape formats for each social platform
- Guest research assistant that briefs the host before recording by summarising a guest's recent writing, talks, and social posts into a set of tailored interview questions
- Cross-episode semantic search enabling listeners and producers to find any statement made across an entire back-catalogue by meaning rather than keyword
